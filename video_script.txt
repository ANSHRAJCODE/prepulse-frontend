================================================================
  PrepPulse — 30 Second Demo Video Script
================================================================

WHAT TO RECORD (use Windows Game Bar: Win + G → Record)
Screen record your browser at https://prepulse-frontend.vercel.app

================================================================
SCENE-BY-SCENE (30 seconds total)
================================================================

[0-4 sec] LANDING PAGE
  - Open https://prepulse-frontend.vercel.app
  - Slowly scroll down to show hero section with dashboard mockup
  - Show the "From Skill Gaps to Placement Offers" heading

[4-10 sec] STUDENT LOGIN + DASHBOARD
  - Click "Get Started" or "Sign In"
  - Click the "Student" demo button (auto-fills credentials)
  - Click Sign In
  - Dashboard loads — show CGPA, skills, recent applications

[10-16 sec] JOB MATCH
  - Click "Browse Jobs" in sidebar
  - Click on any job (e.g. "Software Engineer Backend")
  - Show the match percentage and skill gap section

[16-22 sec] ADMIN DASHBOARD
  - Go back, logout, login as Admin (click Admin demo button)
  - Show the "Placement Command Center"
  - Scroll to show department heatmap and all students table

[22-27 sec] COMPANY VIEW
  - Logout, login as Company (click Company demo button)
  - Show "Recruiter Dashboard" with job postings
  - Click "Candidates" on a job to show ranked student list

[27-30 sec] CLOSE
  - Go back to landing page
  - End on the hero section

================================================================
RECORDING TIPS
================================================================
- Use Chrome browser, zoom to 90% for best view
- Record at 1920x1080 if possible
- Move mouse slowly and deliberately
- Use Win+G (Windows) or OBS for recording
- Keep audio off (no narration needed)
- Trim to exactly 30 seconds using any video editor
  (Windows Photos app can trim videos)
